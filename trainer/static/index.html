How these numbers are made

NFSP frequencies come from a saved 20-chip policy, not a GTO solve. Live action EV estimates sample complete hands against a heuristic continuation with a uniform hidden opponent hand; they are not GTO frequencies or EVs. Solver reviews use illustrative ranges. Flop and turn reviews omit later betting; river reviews use restricted betting.

What this solver supports

River uses a restricted finite CFR+ game. Turn uses exact public-card chance and river betting for at most four compatible deals and 100 iterations. Flop and preflop are unavailable here. Ranges are assumed; they are not inferred from betting history. Reported NashConv measures only the supplied restricted game.
